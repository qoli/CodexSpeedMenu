The response reducer in Sources/SpeedCore/Reducer.swift is a Swift adaptation of
adenta/codex-tps analysis.go, pinned to commit
be549617f50409c8c0e15a8a6838520ec12d2bb1:
https://github.com/adenta/codex-tps/tree/be549617f50409c8c0e15a8a6838520ec12d2bb1

The original MIT license is preserved in LICENSE and in the unmodified upstream
snapshot directory. The synthetic test fixture in Fixtures is used to compare
the Swift reducer with the upstream algorithm; it contains no user conversation.
No upstream CLI, plotting dependency, PNG
report generation or CSV generation is used at runtime.
